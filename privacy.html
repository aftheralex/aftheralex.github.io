Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026. Operator: Aleksandr Isaev (aftheralex@gmail.com).

What the app accesses

Domain Outreach Assistant connects only to the operator's own Gmail account (scope gmail.modify). It sends the operator's business emails, reads replies in the conversations it started, checks the mailbox for an existing conversation before a first contact, and applies its own label. It never deletes, archives or relabels other mail and never downloads attachments.

How the data is used

Email content is used only to run these business conversations. To draft and classify replies, email text may be processed by AI model providers (Anthropic, Ollama) through their APIs. Data is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not used for any other purpose.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on the operator's own computer. Conversations with closed contacts are deleted after 12 months; an opt-out list is kept so that people who asked not to be contacted are never emailed again.

Your choices

Reply "unsubscribe" to any email to stop further messages, or write to aftheralex@gmail.com to request deletion of your data.
